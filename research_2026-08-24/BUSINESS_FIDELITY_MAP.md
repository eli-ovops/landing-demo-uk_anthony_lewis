## Fidelity conclusions

- **Core customer task:** a homeowner evaluates whether the maker covers their type of bespoke room/furniture work, gains confidence from craft/project evidence, then calls or emails to begin a conversation.
- **Minimum accurate slice:** kitchens; bedrooms/dressing rooms; broader bespoke furniture/solutions; Bomere Heath/Shropshire craft context; direct phone/email.
- **Unknowns to preserve:** prices, lead times, service radius, project names, testimonials, awards/certifications, precise installation/design process, materials, guarantees, staff roles (except Council’s named owner), and image rights.
- **Fidelity failure to avoid:** substituting a generic “luxury interiors” page for the demonstrated cabinetmaking/kitchens/bedrooms/bespoke-furniture mix, or using a concept image that implies an actual Anthony Lewis project/workshop.
