# Unique Cold Demo Brief — Anthony Lewis Ltd

**Status:** research brief only. `DEMO AUTHORISATION: NOT GRANTED.` No code, design asset, site, or public preview has been created.

## Design intent

The demo should feel like a quiet Shropshire workshop portfolio, not a generic luxury-interiors landing page: materially grounded, warm and restrained, with one clear path from a homeowner’s room need to a first conversation. Its distinctiveness comes from the verified three-part offer (kitchens; bedrooms/dressing rooms; broader bespoke furniture), the Bomere Heath workshop context and a proof-led close—not from dark “premium” styling, invented superlatives or a stock-heavy gallery.

## Evidence-led proposition and audience

- **Audience:** homeowners seeking handcrafted fitted or room-specific furniture; commercial/trade audience is not evidenced.
- **Hero:** `Bespoke kitchens and furniture, made for your home` (copy must be reconfirmed at production), Anthony Lewis, Bomere Heath / Shropshire context, one direct `Call or email to discuss your project` CTA.
- **Core outcome:** help a visitor understand the range of bespoke work and begin a direct conversation—not booking, pricing, quoting, stock or online purchase.

## Proposed information architecture

1. **Hero** — one dedicated, rights-cleared craft/interior image; Anthony Lewis; concise verified bespoke-work proposition; direct phone/email CTA; natural `Independent concept preview` label if a Demo exists.
2. **Three service slices** — Kitchens; Bedrooms & Dressing Rooms; Bespoke Solutions. Descriptions must not add unverified materials, prices, guarantees or process claims.
3. **Craft reassurance** — short, evidence-based Shropshire cabinetmaker/workshop context. Do not claim service coverage beyond the confirmed workshop location.
4. **Selected work / project proof** — only if individual project image/caption rights are separately reviewed. Otherwise use a non-deceptive information-led composition, not invented case studies or testimonial cards.
5. **Bottom 40% strategy: Reassurance + Specificity + Conversion** — explain that the business covers several home-furniture contexts, restate direct contact for an initial conversation, and end with a workshop/brand-close treatment. This adds new commercial information rather than a generic oversized CTA.
6. **Footer** — public address, phone and `mailto:` email only; no self-hosted contact form, newsletter, tracking or social-feed embed.

## Visual and mobile strategy

- **Visual direction:** natural timber/stone/ink palette, measured serif/sans pairing, generous but not empty spacing, architectural crop of a single real/right-cleared project or an explicitly conceptual non-project atmosphere image.
- **Mobile:** a compact menu or no secondary navigation; all content fits within 390px with no horizontal scroll; Hero CTA remains visible without relying on a slide carousel; service cards stack with readable tap targets.
- **Avoid:** duplicated navigation, hero carousel/repeated headline, decorative Instagram feed, overlaid collage, generic “transform your space” claims, fake showroom/team/project imagery, and a one-button-only ending.
